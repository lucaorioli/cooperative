Italian localization of the cooperator module:

- fiscal code (codice fiscale) on the subscription request, required for
  the subscribers living in Italy, copied to the partner and used to
  find an existing partner;
- Italian legal forms for the companies;
- share capital account in the settings, next to the cooperator account,
  with a warning while it is not set;
- capital release requests excluded from the electronic invoicing (SdI);
- registration in the register of cooperative societies (Albo delle
  società cooperative), shown on the cooperator certificate;
- member categories: category of each share type (cooperator members,
  supporting members - soci sovventori, financing members - soci
  finanziatori), type of cooperator member (worker, user, volunteer),
  special category of art. 2527 of the civil code;
- social cooperatives (type A, B or both, law 381/1991): volunteer
  members allowed only there, and at most half of the members;
- admission date, termination date and reason, encumbrances of each
  member;
- members register (libro soci, art. 2421 of the civil code): for each
  share category the members with fiscal code and address, the
  operations (subscriptions, transfers, sell backs, conversions), the
  shares held, the paid capital, the termination and the encumbrances.

Usage
=====

In *Settings > Cooperators > Accounting* choose:

- the *Cooperator Account*, the receivable of the capital release
  requests (in the Italian balance sheet: "Crediti verso soci per
  versamenti ancora dovuti");
- the *Share Capital Account*, the equity account of the shares
  ("Capitale sociale"). While it is not set the shares are recorded on
  the default income account of the company, as revenue, or the
  validation of the subscription requests stops if the company has no
  default income account.

No account is created by the module: use the accounts of your chart of
accounts.

The registration in the register of cooperative societies and the type
of social cooperative are set in *Settings > Cooperators > Italy*.

The category of each share type (cooperator members, supporting members,
financing members) is set on the share product, in the *Share
Information* tab. The member type is chosen on the subscription request
and copied to the member when the request is validated; it can be
changed on the contact, with the special category, the termination and
the encumbrances.

When a member sells back or transfers all the shares, the termination
date and reason are set from the operation; change the reason when it is
an exclusion or a death. The members register is printed from
*Cooperators > Registers > Cooperator Register*: the list shows the
current members, and also the former ones when the *Current Members*
filter is removed; select the members and print *Cooperator Register*.

Payments collected outside Odoo, for instance withheld from the pay of a
member by an external payroll service, are recorded as a payment of the
member on the receivable account and reconciled with the capital release
request.
