Italian localization of the cooperator module:

- fiscal code (codice fiscale) on the subscription request, required for the
  subscribers living in Italy, copied to the partner and used to find an
  existing partner;
- Italian legal forms for the companies;
- share capital account in the settings, next to the cooperator account,
  with a warning while it is not set;
- capital release requests excluded from the electronic invoicing (SdI);
- registration in the register of cooperative societies (Albo delle società
  cooperative), shown on the cooperator certificate;
- member categories: category of each share type (cooperator members,
  supporting members - soci sovventori, financing members - soci
  finanziatori), type of cooperator member (worker, user, volunteer), special
  category of art. 2527 of the civil code;
- social cooperatives (type A, B or both, law 381/1991): volunteer members
  allowed only there, and at most half of the members;
- admission date, termination date and reason, encumbrances of each member;
- members register (libro soci, art. 2421 of the civil code): for each share
  category the members with fiscal code and address, the operations
  (subscriptions, transfers, sell backs, conversions), the shares held, the
  paid capital, the termination and the encumbrances.
