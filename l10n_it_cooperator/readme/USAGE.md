In *Settings > Cooperators > Accounting* choose:

- the *Cooperator Account*, the receivable of the capital release requests
  (in the Italian balance sheet: "Crediti verso soci per versamenti ancora
  dovuti");
- the *Share Capital Account*, the equity account of the shares ("Capitale
  sociale"). While it is not set the shares are recorded on the default income
  account of the company, as revenue, or the validation of the subscription
  requests stops if the company has no default income account.

No account is created by the module: use the accounts of your chart of
accounts.

The registration in the register of cooperative societies and the type of
social cooperative are set in *Settings > Cooperators > Italy*.

The category of each share type (cooperator members, supporting members,
financing members) is set on the share product, in the *Share Information*
tab. The member type is chosen on the subscription request and copied to the
member when the request is validated; it can be changed on the contact, with
the special category, the termination and the encumbrances.

When a member sells back or transfers all the shares, the termination date and
reason are set from the operation; change the reason when it is an exclusion
or a death. The members register is printed from *Cooperators > Registers > Cooperator
Register*: the list shows the current members, and also the former ones when
the *Current Members* filter is removed; select the members and print
*Cooperator Register*.

Payments collected outside Odoo, for instance withheld from the pay of a
member by an external payroll service, are recorded as a payment of the member
on the receivable account and reconciled with the capital release request.
