Register of the corporate bodies of a company and of their office
holders:

- body types (general meeting, board of directors, sole director,
  supervisory board, statutory auditor, committee, ...), each with the
  offices its members can hold;
- offices (chair, vice-chair, chief executive officer, member,
  secretary, ...);
- corporate bodies, with their start date, expected end of term, term of
  office as stated in the appointment and state: in office, extended
  (the term has expired but the body stays in office until the new one
  is appointed) or ceased;
- members of each body, with their own appointment and end dates (a
  member can be co-opted or resign during the term of the body), end
  reason, legal representation, powers and delegations;
- current and past offices of each contact, on the contact form.

The module is generic: it can be used by any kind of company, not only
by cooperatives.

Usage
=====

1. Give the users the *Governance / User* (read only) or *Governance /
   Administrator* access right.
2. In *Governance > Configuration*, review the body types and the
   offices.
3. In *Governance > Corporate Bodies*, create a body, set its type and
   start date, and add its members with their offices.
4. When the term expires and the body stays in office until the new one
   is appointed, click *Extend*. When the new body is appointed, click
   *Cease* on the old one: the members still in office leave with it.
5. *Governance > Office Holders* lists the current offices; remove the
   *Current* filter to see the past ones. The contact form shows the
   offices of the contact in the *Corporate Offices* tab.
