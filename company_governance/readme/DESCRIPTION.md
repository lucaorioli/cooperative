Register of the corporate bodies of a company and of their office holders:

- body types (general meeting, board of directors, sole director,
  supervisory board, statutory auditor, committee, ...), each with the
  offices its members can hold;
- offices (chair, vice-chair, chief executive officer, member, secretary,
  ...);
- corporate bodies, with their start date, expected end of term, term of
  office as stated in the appointment and state: in office, extended (the
  term has expired but the body stays in office until the new one is
  appointed) or ceased;
- members of each body, with their own appointment and end dates (a member
  can be co-opted or resign during the term of the body), end reason, legal
  representation, powers and delegations;
- current and past offices of each contact, on the contact form.

The module is generic: it can be used by any kind of company, not only by
cooperatives.
