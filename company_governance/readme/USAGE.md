1. Give the users the *Governance / User* (read only) or *Governance /
   Administrator* access right.
2. In *Governance > Configuration*, review the body types and the offices.
3. In *Governance > Corporate Bodies*, create a body, set its type and start
   date, and add its members with their offices.
4. When the term expires and the body stays in office until the new one is
   appointed, click *Extend*. When the new body is appointed, click *Cease*
   on the old one: the members still in office leave with it.
5. *Governance > Office Holders* lists the current offices; remove the
   *Current* filter to see the past ones. The contact form shows the offices
   of the contact in the *Corporate Offices* tab.
