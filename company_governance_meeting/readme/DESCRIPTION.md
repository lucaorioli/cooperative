Meetings of the corporate bodies managed by *Company Governance*, with
their resolutions and minutes:

- meetings of a body: ordinary or extraordinary, convocation date and
  method, date and place, agenda, chair and secretary, state (draft,
  convened, held, minuted, cancelled);
- attendees: present, represented by a proxy or absent, with their votes
  (one head, one vote, or the share of the capital), and the quorum of
  the people and votes present or represented;
- resolutions: subject, text, outcome and votes for, against and
  abstentions, numbered by year when the meeting is held;
- appointing and ending resolution of the bodies and of their members;
- minutes: text, signed copy attached, and printed minutes.
