1. In *Governance > Meetings*, create a meeting of a body, with its date,
   agenda and convocation, and click *Convene*.
2. In the *Attendees* tab, click *Load Members* to add the current
   members of the body, then set who is present, represented or absent
   and their votes. The quorum is shown below the list.
3. In the *Resolutions* tab, add the resolutions with their outcome and
   votes. Click *Mark as Held*: the resolutions get their number.
4. Write the minutes, or attach the signed copy, and click *Mark as
   Minuted*. *Print > Minutes* prints them.
5. On a body or a member, set the resolution that appointed or ended it.
